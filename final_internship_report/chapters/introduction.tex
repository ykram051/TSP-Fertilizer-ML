\chapter{Introduction}\label{ch:intro}

\section{Context and motivation}
Triple Super Phosphate (TSP) is a concentrated phosphate fertilizer obtained by reacting ground phosphate rock with phosphoric acid. In the TSP line studied during this internship, a slurry is formed, sprayed and dried, and its \emph{free acid} content, recorded under the tag \var{SLURRY\_FREE\_ACID}, is the quantity the project tries to estimate and anticipate. The plant historian provides one-minute measurements of flows, temperatures, pressures and levels, but the free-acid value is a quality-type measurement that is not necessarily available as a continuous online signal.

Two operational questions follow. First, can the free acid be \emph{estimated now} from process variables alone when no fresh measurement exists (virtual sensing)? Second, when a recent laboratory result \emph{is} available, can its short-term movement be \emph{anticipated} earlier than by waiting for the next result (forecasting)? These problems allow different inputs and must not be mixed: presenting a current estimate as an early warning, or a laboratory value as a live sensor, would overstate what any model can do.

\section{Objectives}
The general objective was a transparent, reproducible and strictly advisory prototype for free-acid monitoring. It was broken down into six specific objectives:
\begin{enumerate}
\item characterise the historian extract (cadence, gaps, extremes, variable semantics);
\item build a leakage-controlled, time-respecting feature contract (Chapter~\ref{ch:data});
\item compare baselines and models under chronological validation (Chapters~\ref{ch:method} and~\ref{ch:results});
\item separate future forecasting from current-time virtual sensing;
\item assess robustness across chronological folds and select a model on stability, not only on mean error;
\item connect the saved artifacts to a read-only streaming architecture with a monitoring dashboard (Chapter~\ref{ch:deploy}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Main contributions}
The work produced (i) a documented feature contract of 178 engineered features per sample built from 21 sensors; (ii) a target-anchored delta forecast whose RMSE beats the persistence baseline by 19.8\% to 34.9\% on the held-out period (Table~\ref{tab:forecast}); (iii) a target-free virtual sensor with an empirical 90\% interval and an out-of-envelope flag; (iv) a stability-oriented selection of a five-cluster Ridge model; and (v) a Docker/OPC UA replay prototype with data-quality shielding, SQLite audit storage and a native desktop dashboard.
