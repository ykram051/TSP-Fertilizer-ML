\chapter{Internship Context and Problem Definition}
\section{TSP monitoring context}
The confirmed target is \texttt{SLURRY\_FREE\_ACID}. Consequently, the project separates three contracts: direct future-level prediction, target-anchored forecasting and target-free current-time estimation. This separation is central to the validity of the conclusions.

Direct future-level prediction uses process information available at time $t$ to estimate $y_{t+h}$. Target-anchored forecasting is allowed to use a fresh current laboratory result $y_t$ and estimates its future change. Target-free virtual sensing estimates the present value from process variables only. The latter is not an early-warning forecast.

\begin{table}[H]\centering
\caption{Operational prediction contracts evaluated in the project.}\label{tab:contracts}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{L{0.25\textwidth}Y Y}\toprule
\textbf{Contract} & \textbf{Inputs} & \textbf{Output}\\\midrule
Direct level & Process measurements at or before $t$ & $\hat y_{t+h}$\\
Target anchored & Process information and fresh $y_t$ & $\hat y_{t+h}$ through predicted change\\
Target free & Process information only & Current estimate $\hat y_t$\\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

Table~\ref{tab:contracts} prevents a common industrial error: presenting a current estimate as a future warning, or using a laboratory value as though it were a live sensor. The constraints also include time ordering, missing data, unknown unit semantics for several tags, limited data coverage, and the requirement that a model failure must not affect plant control.

\section{Evolution of the work}
The work evolved from exploration to feature preparation, direct benchmarks, delta forecasting, a target-free virtual sensor, robustness research, dashboard development and a replay-based integration prototype. The final deployment code retains the target-free five-cluster Ridge candidate as a stability-oriented model. The target-anchored Ridge delta model remains a separate configurable mode. Neither is presented as a closed-loop controller or as fully validated on future production periods.
