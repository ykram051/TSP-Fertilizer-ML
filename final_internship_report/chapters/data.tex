\chapter{Data Understanding and Preparation}
\section{Data source and scope}
The notebooks use a confidential historian export named \texttt{tsp\_1min.csv}. The raw file is intentionally absent from the repository, but generated audit artifacts record 44,640 one-minute rows from 1 to 31 January 2026. It contains 21 numeric process measurements and the target. There are 308 missing target values. Data were sorted by the \texttt{Date} timestamp, and all evaluation splits preserve chronology.

\section{Data quality and preprocessing}
The exploratory notebook examined timestamp order, cadence, duplicates, missingness, descriptive statistics, target extremes, correlations and time evolution. The target had 308 missing values and showed unusually extreme observations. For example, the observed minimum and maximum were 3.392 and 52.671, while the central distribution was concentrated near 11.3. These observations motivated diagnostics and conservative preprocessing, not automatic removal of all unusual values.

The structural warm-up removes the first 30 rows because required lagged and rolling history does not exist. Later isolated sensor gaps remain part of the chronology. The pipeline adds missingness indicators and uses training-fitted median imputation. Process-derived values use an $\epsilon=10^{-6}$ denominator guard, replace infinities with missing values, and are clipped to training-period 0.1\textsuperscript{th} and 99.9\textsuperscript{th} percentiles. These rules prevent unstable ratios from dominating a model while avoiding validation or test fitting.

\section{Feature engineering}
For each process source, accepted temporal features include current value, selected lags, 10- and 30-minute rolling means, a 10-minute rolling standard deviation, a five-minute difference and a 10-minute rolling slope. Lags were selected from 1, 5, 10, 15, 30 and 60 minutes using training-only association, mutual information, stability across chronological blocks and a limited process-response prior. At most three lags were retained per source.

The implementation also creates total acid flow, acid-flow imbalance, acid-to-phosphate ratio, total slurry volumetric flow, a slurry-density proxy, thermal gradients, a steam-condition proxy, drying and recycle proxies, and selected flow coefficients of variation. Only the two slurry volumetric components are confirmed additive in the registry. The other derived relationships are explicitly treated as proxies.
