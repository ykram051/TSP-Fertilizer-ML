\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}\label{ch:conclusion}
This internship investigated free-acid monitoring for a TSP process from one month of one-minute data. The work moved from exploration and leakage-controlled feature engineering to benchmarks, target-anchored delta forecasting, target-free virtual sensing, fold-based stability research, and an advisory Docker/OPC UA prototype with a native dashboard.

The main forecasting result is a hybrid-feature Ridge delta model that beat persistence at 1, 5, 10 and 15 minutes, with RMSE improvements between 19.8\% and 34.9\% on the held-out period. A separate target-free Ridge--LightGBM virtual sensor outperformed the constant baseline (RMSE 0.301 against 0.454), and five-cluster Ridge was retained by the runtime for its stability across folds. These results answer different questions and have different evidence levels, so they are not combined (Table~\ref{tab:summary}).

Future work follows the roadmap of Chapter~\ref{ch:deploy}. On the modelling side, it consists of evaluating the frozen candidates on further months with rolling-origin validation, confirming the regime-aware hybrid, calibrating the uncertainty on independent residuals and relating the statistical clusters to operating records. On the deployment side, the next milestones are a secured Kepware connection, historian backfill, a retention and backup policy and a longer read-only shadow operation with operator feedback (Stage~2), followed by the advisory display (Stage~3).
