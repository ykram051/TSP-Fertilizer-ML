\textbf{Abstract.}

This report documents a three-month machine-learning project carried out at OCP Group for monitoring and anticipating \texttt{SLURRY\_FREE\_ACID} in a TSP production process. The work addresses two complementary tasks: estimating the current free-acid value from process measurements when no recent laboratory result is available, and forecasting its short-term evolution when a recent target measurement can be used as an anchor. One month of one-minute historian data was analysed using a leakage-controlled and time-respecting methodology, including temporal and process features, chronological train/validation/test splits, persistence baselines, and stability analysis across expanding validation folds. A target-anchored delta model achieved a 19.8\%--34.9\% RMSE improvement over persistence for horizons of 1 to 15 minutes, while the target-free virtual sensor achieved an RMSE of 0.301 on the held-out period. The selected models were integrated into an advisory-only Docker and OPC UA replay prototype with data-quality checks, audit storage and a read-only monitoring dashboard. The prototype was evaluated in a laboratory replay environment; multi-month validation and read-only shadow operation on the plant network remain necessary before any operational use.
